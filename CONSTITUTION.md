# Market Pilot Crypto Constitution

## Article 1
Market Pilot Crypto exists to discover quantitative alpha and execute paper trading with realistic microstructure economics.

## Article 2
Paper-only foundation. Zero live exchange execution under any circumstances (INV-CRYPTO-001). Complete isolation from the Indian equities/options desk (INV-CRYPTO-010).

## Article 3
Every alpha candidate and rejection must be explainable and preserved with full mathematical negative proofs (INV-CRYPTO-013).

## Article 4
Production stability, capital preservation, and mathematical positive expectancy are more important than forcing trade frequency. Never relax thresholds without regime-conditional evidence.

## Article 5
Documentation is part of the product. `CRYPTO_LOGBOOK.md` is mandatory reference reading before any analysis or code proposal.

## Article 6
Every AI assistant must follow `AGENTS.md` and the Mandatory 3-Stage Gatekeeper Process. Zero direct code changes or file mutations on Turn 1 without explicit human user approval.

## Article 7
Every architectural and quantitative calibration must be empirically audited across live data and documented with exact data citations.

## Article 8
Code must be modular, reusable, typed, and 100% verified against quantitative and risk invariants before committing.

## Article 9
INV-OPS-001: The AI agent NEVER executes deployment commands on Oracle VM 1 or Oracle VM 2. User executes all deployments.

## Article 10
Zero hardcoded currency constants. Position sizing, stop losses, profit targets, and trailing ratchets must be derived dynamically from account equity risk budgets and full round-trip friction.
